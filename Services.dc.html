Services

Practical safety support, end to end

From a single site audit to building a full management system from scratch — every service is delivered on site, not from a template.

01

ISO 45001 H&S management system audits

Independent audits of your health & safety management system against ISO 45001, with a clear, prioritised action list.

02

H&S management system & policy review

Review of and hands-on assistance building your health & safety management system and policies, so they match how your business actually operates.

03

Risk assessment & method statement review

RAMS reviewed before work starts, checked against the actual site conditions — not just the template.

04

Comprehensive site audits & inspections

Site audits including full site inspection, delivered with a written report and a prioritised close-out list.

05

Work at height equipment inspection

Thorough inspection of work at height equipment, with a certificate issued on completion — including recertification when it falls due.

06

Fire risk assessments & fire safety

Fire risk assessments and ongoing fire safety services to keep your site or premises compliant.

07

Accident, incident & near miss investigation

Investigation of accidents, incidents and near misses, including RIDDOR reporting where required.

08

Health & safety training

Training including manual handling, abrasive wheel, PPE and work at height, delivered on site for your team.

09

Display screen equipment (DSE) assessments

Workstation assessments to meet DSE regulations for office and site-based staff alike.

10

COSHH assessments

Assessments of substances hazardous to health, with practical control measures your team can actually follow.

11

Qualitative face fit testing

Face fit testing to confirm RPE is providing the protection it's rated for, for each wearer.

12

Portable appliance testing (PAT)

PAT testing of portable electrical equipment on site or in the office.

13

SSIP & ISO certification support

Hands-on help preparing for and maintaining CHAS, SMAS Worksafe, Constructionline, ISO 45001 and ISO 9001. See accreditation support for the full process.
